# Who owns what

**YWCA's name, logo, and brand belong to YWCA.** This repo does not grant anyone the right to use them. The two logo files in `assets/logos/` are here so YWCA Knoxville & the Tennessee Valley staff and their volunteers can make YWCA materials. If you are not making something for YWCA, do not use them.

**The rules in this kit restate YWCA's own brand guide** (the 2018 YWCA USA brand guide) and what YWCA's two public websites do. The wording here is the volunteers' summary. It is not YWCA's official guidance.

**The rest was written by volunteers** at Hack for Nonprofits 2026: the token files, the component markup and CSS, the Power BI theme, the build script, and the documentation. It was made for YWCA to use, change, and keep.

No open-source license has been chosen for this repo yet. Until one is, ask before reusing it outside YWCA: open an issue on this repo.

**No photos are included.** See `assets/photos/README.md`.

**No resident data is in this repo, and none ever should be.** Every number in a component preview is invented.
